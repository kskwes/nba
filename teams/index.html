ORtg / DRtg / NET・PACE
ORtg / DRtg / NET = pts scored / allowed / diff per 100 poss. PACE = poss. per game.
Compares offense and defense with game tempo evened out.
Possessions are ESPN estimates.
TS% / eFG%
TS% = shooting efficiency incl. FT. eFG% = FG% with a 3 counted as 1.5.
TOV% / ORB%
TOV% = share of plays ending in a turnover (lower is better). ORB% = share of available offensive rebounds grabbed.
